# Privacy Policy for Ops Qr

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Ops Qr ("the app") is a QR code generator and scanner. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

## Information we collect
Ops Qr does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. The app does not require an account, and we do not run any servers that receive your data.

## Camera
The app asks for camera permission only so it can scan QR codes in real time. Camera images are processed on your device and are never saved, uploaded, or shared.

## QR code content
Text, links, and other content that you scan or generate stays on your device. The app does not transmit it to us or to any third party.

## Third-party services
OpsQr does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking services. If a scanned QR code contains a link and you choose to open it, it opens in your browser or another app, which is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

## Children's privacy
The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Data security
Because your data stays on your device, it is protected by your device's own security.

## Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
